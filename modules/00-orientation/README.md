# Module 00 — Orientation

Status: draft
Time: 9:00–9:20 (20 min — lecture 8 / activity 12)
Objective: none (sets up the day)

## Purpose

Get everyone logged in, paired, and holding an idea to work on. Set the one
rule that matters all day: nothing confidential goes into the tools. This isn't
the place for content.

## Outline

| Min | Segment | What happens | Who's talking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | Welcome | Instructor intro; housekeeping (Wi-Fi, breaks at 10:20 and 14:05, lunch at 11:50, end at 16:00) | Instructor |
| 3–8 | The day | The four things you'll be able to do by 16:00 (plain-language objectives); the arc (practice case this morning, your own idea this afternoon); what you'll take home (a tested prompt template and a one-page reference); **the data rule**; what's out of scope | Instructor |
| 8–13 | Pre-check | 5-question pre-check ([assessment/pre-post-check.md](../../assessment/pre-post-check.md)), ungraded and anonymous | Participants |
| 13–18 | Pairs and ideas | Pair up, ideally with someone from a different field. Each person writes **their idea in one sentence** on an index card and tells their partner: name, what you do, the idea | Participants |
| 18–20 | Tech check | Everyone opens their AI assistant and sends "hello". Hands up if it doesn't work | Participants |

## Key points

1. By 16:00 you'll have a prompt template you've tested on your own work, not
   just notes.
2. **Data rule:** if you wouldn't post it on a public website, don't paste it
   into an AI tool you haven't been cleared to use. Describe ideas in general
   terms.
3. Every technique today works on any major assistant, free tier included.
   Tool choice matters less than how you ask.

## Materials

- [ ] Welcome slide: Wi-Fi details, schedule, data rule
- [ ] Pre-check (paper or form link)
- [ ] Index cards and markers for the idea sentence
- [ ] Link to the shared prompt document (open-questions Q16)
- [ ] Fallback ideas for anyone without one: *a service you wish existed in
      your neighbourhood*, *a frustrating process at work*, or Loop Laundry

## Common problems and what to do

| Problem | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Late arrivals | Start at 9:00 anyway. Latecomers skip the pre-check; don't restart |
| No AI account, or login fails | Pair them with someone whose tool works; fix it at the 10:20 break. Morning labs are in pairs, so nobody is blocked |
| "I don't have an idea" | Hand them the fallback list. A small idea works fine; the skill transfers |
| "Which tool is best?" | "Whichever you have. We'll see differences in Lab 01, and they matter less than you'd expect" |

## If running behind

1. Skip spoken intros; ideas go on cards only (saves ~3 min)
2. Move the pre-check to a link completed during Lab 01 step 1 (saves 5 min)
