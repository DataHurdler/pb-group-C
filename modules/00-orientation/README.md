# Module 00 — Orientation

Status: draft
Time: 9:00–9:20 (20 min — lecture 8 / activity 12)
Objective: none (sets up the day)

## Purpose

Get everyone logged in, paired, and holding a real task from their work to
improve. Set the one rule that matters all day: know what data you're allowed
to put into which tool. This isn't the place for content.

## Outline

| Min | Segment | What happens | Who's talking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | Welcome | Instructor intro; housekeeping (Wi-Fi, breaks at 10:20 and 14:05, lunch at 11:50, end at 16:00) | Instructor |
| 3–8 | The day | The four things you'll be able to do by 16:00 (plain-language objectives); the arc (shared business case this morning, your own work this afternoon); what you'll take home (a tested prompt template, a one-page use-case plan, and a reference sheet); **the data-and-policy rule**; what's out of scope | Instructor |
| 8–13 | Pre-check | 5-question pre-check ([assessment/pre-post-check.md](../../assessment/pre-post-check.md)), ungraded and anonymous | Participants |
| 13–18 | Pairs and tasks | Pair up, ideally with someone from a different field or business. Each person writes on an index card **one task or process from their work they'd like AI to help with**, and tells their partner: name, what you do, the task | Participants |
| 18–20 | Tech check | Everyone opens their AI assistant and sends "hello". Hands up if it doesn't work | Participants |

## Key points

1. By 16:00 you'll have a tested prompt and a plan to pilot it on your own work
   next week.
2. **Data-and-policy rule:** if you wouldn't post it on a public website, don't
   paste it into an AI tool your organization hasn't approved for that data.
   If you don't know your employer's AI policy, finding out is a good first
   step. Today, describe your work in general terms.
3. Everything today works on any major assistant, free tier included. Which
   tool matters less than how you use it.

## Common problems and what to do

| Problem | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Late arrivals | Start at 9:00 anyway. Latecomers skip the pre-check; don't restart |
| No AI account, or login fails | Pair them with someone whose tool works; fix it at the 10:20 break. Morning labs are in pairs, so nobody is blocked |
| "I can't think of a task" | Hand them the fallback list. Everyone has a weekly chore |
| "My company bans AI tools" | Today uses sample data and general descriptions, so nothing breaks the rule. Their capstone can be a proposal for an approved pilot |
| "Which tool is best?" | "Whichever you have, or whichever your employer approves. We'll see differences in Lab 01, and they matter less than you'd expect" |
