# Sources

Every source cited in this repo, what it supports, and where it's cited. Each
was checked on **2026-10-16** against the publisher's page or a primary record
(see [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)). Vendor documentation pages are undated and
change over time, so recheck them before each offering.

## How AI models work

| Source | What it supports | Cited in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anthropic. *Glossary*. Claude Docs. <https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/glossary> | Models are pretrained "to predict the next word, given the previous context"; the context window is the model's "working memory"; temperature and non-determinism mean identical inputs can produce different outputs | Module 01, reference handout, open questions |
| OpenAI. 2023. *GPT-4 Technical Report*. arXiv:2303.08774. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08774> | "GPT-4 is a Transformer-based model pre-trained to predict the next token in a document"; the model lacks knowledge of events after its pre-training data cutoff | Module 01 |
| OpenAI. 2024 (updated 2024–2025). *Memory and new controls for ChatGPT*. <https://openai.com/index/memory-and-new-controls-for-chatgpt/> | ChatGPT can remember details across conversations when memory is on | Module 01, pre/post answer key |
| Chen, L., Zaharia, M., & Zou, J. 2024. *How Is ChatGPT's Behavior Changing over Time?* Harvard Data Science Review. <https://doi.org/10.1162/99608f92.5317da47> | The same named model changed measurably between March and June 2023; e.g. GPT-4's accuracy at identifying prime numbers fell from 84% to 51% | Module 01, Module 03, Lab 03, prep checklist |

## Hallucination, confidence, and agreeableness

| Source | What it supports | Cited in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walters, W. H., & Wilder, E. I. 2023. *Fabrication and errors in the bibliographic citations generated by ChatGPT*. Scientific Reports 13, 14045. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-41032-5> | 55% of GPT-3.5's and 18% of GPT-4's citations in generated literature reviews were fabricated | Module 01, reference handout, pre/post answer key |
| Kalai, A. T., Nachum, O., Vempala, S. S., & Zhang, E. 2025. *Why Language Models Hallucinate*. arXiv:2509.04664. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04664> | Standard training and evaluation reward guessing over admitting uncertainty, so models produce plausible falsehoods | Module 01, Module 03 |
| Xiong, M., et al. 2024. *Can LLMs Express Their Uncertainty? An Empirical Evaluation of Confidence Elicitation in LLMs*. ICLR 2024. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13063> | "LLMs, when verbalizing their confidence, tend to be overconfident" | Lab 03 |
| Sharma, M., et al. 2024. *Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models*. ICLR 2024. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548> | AI assistants consistently show sycophancy, matching user views rather than being truthful | Objectives, Module 03 |

## AI and work

| Source | What it supports | Cited in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dell'Acqua, F., et al. 2023. *Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier: Field Experimental Evidence of the Effects of AI on Knowledge Worker Productivity and Quality*. HBS working paper; published in Organization Science. <https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.2025.21838> | 758 BCG consultants. On tasks inside the frontier, GPT-4 users completed 12.2% more tasks, 25.1% faster, with higher quality. On a task outside it, they were less likely to be correct (19 percentage points in the working paper; the journal abstract reports 19%) | Module 01 |
| Noy, S., & Zhang, W. 2023. *Experimental evidence on the productivity effects of generative artificial intelligence*. The authors didn't fact-check outputs | Module 01, Module 03 |
| METR (Becker, J., et al.). 2025. *Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity*. <https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/> | Developers expected AI to speed them up by 24%; measured, they took 19% longer; afterwards they still believed it had sped them up by 20% | Module 04, Lab 04 |
| Bloomberg News. 2023-05-02. *Samsung Bans Staff's AI Use After Spotting ChatGPT Data Leak*. <https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-05-02/samsung-bans-chatgpt-and-other-generative-ai-use-by-staff-after-leak> | Samsung restricted generative-AI use after staff uploaded sensitive code to ChatGPT | Module 03, pre/post answer key |

## Not sourced: design judgments

These are deliberate choices, not findings, and are written as such in the
materials: the 6-hour shape and break timing, the 25/75 lecture/activity split,
the six-part brief as a teaching frame, the analyze/communicate/innovate

evidence.
