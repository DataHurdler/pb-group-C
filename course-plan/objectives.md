## How these were written

One objective per content module, four in total. Each starts with an
observable verb, fits inside its module's minutes, and names how it's checked
during the day. The audience is entrepreneurs and professionals, so every
objective is practised on innovation work: ideas, customers, plans, and pitches.

## The objectives

*By the end of the day, participants will be able to…*

| ID | Objective | Taught in | Checked by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LO1 | **Explain** why an AI assistant produced a given output, in terms of prediction and context, and **flag** claims that are likely invented | Module 01 | Lab 01 step 4 (claim sorting); pre/post item 1 |
| LO2 | **Write** a prompt that specifies goal, context, audience, constraints, and format, and **diagnose** which of these is missing from a weak prompt | Module 02 | Lab 02 steps 1–2; pre/post items 2–3 |
| LO3 | **Apply** ideation, simulated-customer, and pre-mortem prompts to their own idea, and **verify** at least two factual claims the model makes | Module 03 | Lab 03 prompt log; pre/post item 4 |
| LO4 | **Evaluate** output against criteria written before testing, **refine** one change at a time, and **package** the result as a reusable template with stated limits | Module 04 | Capstone template + partner test; pre/post item 5 |

## Threads that run through every module

These aren't separate objectives. They're taught where they come up.

- **Data care.** Never paste confidential, client, or personal data into a tool
  you haven't been cleared to use. Stated in Module 00 and repeated in 03.
- **Simulated isn't real.** AI-played customers, investors, or experts generate
  hypotheses, not evidence. Module 03.
- **You own the decision.** The model drafts and challenges; it doesn't decide.
  Module 04 ("don't use this for…").

## Out of scope

Say so when these come up, and point to further resources in the wrap-up.

- Building AI products, APIs, agents, automation tools (Zapier, Make, etc.), or code
- Fine-tuning, training, or how models are built beyond what prompting needs
- Image, video, and audio generation
- Tool-by-tool feature comparisons and pricing
- Legal questions about AI (IP ownership, regulation): flag them and refer out
