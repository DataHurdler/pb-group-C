## How these were written

One objective per content module, four in total. Each starts with an
observable verb, fits inside its module's minutes, and names how it's checked
during the day. The course has a business focus, so every objective is
practised on workplace tasks: analysis, communication, ideas, and recurring
processes.

## The objectives

*By the end of the day, participants will be able to…*

| ID | Objective | Taught in | Checked by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LO1 | **Explain** why an AI assistant produced a given output, in terms of prediction and context; **flag** claims that are likely invented; and **sort** workplace tasks into good fit / use with care / not for AI | Module 01 | Lab 01 steps 3–4; pre/post items 1–2 |
| LO2 | **Write** a prompt that specifies goal, context, audience, constraints, and format, and **diagnose** which of these is missing from a weak prompt | Module 02 | Lab 02 steps 1–2; pre/post item 3 |
| LO3 | **Apply** AI to an analysis, communication, or idea-development task, and **verify** the output (numbers, facts, tone) before relying on it | Module 03 | Lab 03 track output + verification step; pre/post item 4 |
| LO4 | **Design** an AI use case for a recurring task: test a prompt against criteria written in advance, refine one change at a time, and complete a one-page plan covering value, risk, and data handling | Module 04 | Use-case canvas + partner test; pre/post item 5 |

## Threads that run through every module

These aren't separate objectives. They're taught where they come up.

- **Data and policy.** Know your employer's AI policy. Never paste
  confidential, client, or personal data into a tool you haven't been cleared
  to use. Stated in Module 00, applied in Module 03 (the analysis track uses
  sample data for this reason), and built into the Module 04 canvas.
- **You're accountable for the output.** If it goes out under your name, you
  checked it. Module 03.
- **Simulated isn't real.** AI-played customers or experts generate
  hypotheses, not evidence. Module 03, innovation track.
- **Value is measurable.** A use case worth adopting saves time or improves
  quality in a way you can name. Module 04.

## Out of scope

Say so when these come up, and point to further resources in the wrap-up.

- Building AI products, APIs, agents, automation platforms (Zapier, Make,
  etc.), or code
- Choosing or buying enterprise AI tools; vendor comparisons and pricing
- Writing an organization's AI policy (we cover what to ask, not what to decide)
- Image, video, and audio generation
- Legal questions about AI (IP ownership, regulation): flag them and refer out
- AI's effect on jobs and the economy, beyond a brief acknowledgment if asked
