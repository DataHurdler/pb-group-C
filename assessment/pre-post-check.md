# Pre/Post Check

Status: draft. Items to be piloted on 2–3 people before the day

Given at 9:08 (Module 00) and again at 15:45 (Module 05). Same five items both
times. Ungraded and anonymous; participants write the same self-chosen code
word on both so the two can be paired.

**Time limit:** 5 minutes.

---

**Code word:** ____________

**1.** You ask an AI assistant for the size of the e-bike rental market in your
city. It gives a precise figure and names a research firm as the source. What's
the best way to treat this?

- A. Use it. It named a source
- B. Treat it as a plausible guess until you've found the source yourself
- C. Use it if you're on a paid version of the tool
- D. Ignore it. AI can't handle numbers

**2.** A colleague wants to use a free AI chatbot to summarize a client's
contract before a meeting. Your company hasn't said anything about AI tools.
What's the best advice?

- A. Go ahead. The summary will save time
- B. Go ahead, but delete the chat afterwards
- C. Check whether the contract is confidential and what the company allows
  before pasting it in; otherwise summarize from general, non-identifying details
- D. Never use AI for anything involving clients

**3.** Which change to *"Write a pitch for my app"* is most likely to improve the
result?

- A. Starting with "You are a world-class pitch expert"
- B. Adding who the pitch is for, the app's key facts, the length, and the format
- C. Adding "Please be detailed and thorough"
- D. Sending the same prompt three times and picking the best

**4.** You paste a year of monthly sales into an AI assistant. It says revenue
grew 18% and that the growth was caused by your new website. What's the best
next step?

- A. Put both findings in your report. It analyzed the actual data
- B. Recalculate the 18% yourself, and treat "caused by the website" as a
  hypothesis the data alone can't confirm
- C. Ask it the same question again to see if it gives the same answer
- D. Discard both. AI can't analyze data

**5.** You changed a prompt's audience, length limit, and example all at once,
and the output improved. What's the main problem?

- A. There's no problem. It improved
- B. You can't tell which change helped, so you can't reliably reuse what you
  learned
- C. The model will now remember your preferences and overfit to them
- D. The prompt is now too long

---

## Answer key (instructor only)

| # | Objective | Answer | Why the wrong options are tempting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | LO1 | B | A: a named source *feels* like evidence, but models fabricate citations. C: newer models invent less but still do: in one study 55% of GPT-3.5's and 18% of GPT-4's citations were fabricated ([Walters & Wilder, 2023, *Fabrication and errors in the bibliographic citations generated by ChatGPT*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-41032-5)) |
| 2 | LO1 | C | A is tempting because it saves time; Samsung's 2023 restriction followed staff uploading sensitive code ([Bloomberg, 2023, *Samsung Bans Staff's AI Use After Spotting ChatGPT Data Leak*](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-05-02/samsung-bans-chatgpt-and-other-generative-ai-use-by-staff-after-leak)). B sounds responsible but doesn't undo sharing the data. D is overcautious and unhelpful |
| 3 | LO2 | B | A is a widely shared tip, but adding a persona didn't improve factual accuracy in [Zheng et al., 2024, *When "A Helpful Assistant" Is Not Really Helpful*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.10054) |
| 4 | LO3 | B | A: "it used my data" feels like accuracy, but arithmetic and causal claims still need checking. C: consistency isn't correctness |
| 5 | LO4 | B | A feels reasonable because the result improved. C: some assistants do have memory features ([OpenAI, 2024, *Memory and new controls for ChatGPT*](https://openai.com/index/memory-and-new-controls-for-chatgpt/)), but that isn't the problem here |

## Reading the results

- Compare the share correct per item, pre vs. post.
- An item most people get right in the **pre**-check is too easy; replace it.
  We expect item 3 may be too easy for an enthusiast audience; the pilot will
  tell.
- An item that stays low in the **post**-check points to the module that needs
  rework.
