# Instructions for AI assistants working in this repo

## All responses must be based in fact, with sources cited

This applies to every response and to everything written into this repo:
course materials, handouts, instructor notes, and replies in chat.

- **Every factual claim needs a source.** This includes statistics, research
  findings (for example, on attention, learning, or retention), claims about how
  AI models behave, and claims about tools, products, or policies. Cite the
  source inline, with author or organization, title, year, and a URL or DOI
  where one exists.
- **Never invent a source.** Cite only sources you are confident exist and
  that support the specific claim. If you can't name one, don't make one up.
- **If you can't source it, say so.** Either remove the claim or mark it
  `[unverified]` and flag it in your reply so a person can check it.
- **Separate fact from judgment.** Design choices, recommendations, and
  teaching opinions don't need citations, but they must read as judgment
  ("we recommend…", "in our experience…"), not as established fact.
- **Label invented material.** Practice cases, sample data, and example
  prompts are fictional by design. Keep them clearly marked as invented (see
  [labs/practice-case.md](labs/practice-case.md)) and never cite them as
  evidence.
