# Capstone Brief — Design Your AI Use Case

**14:30–15:25 · 55 minutes · individually, then swap with your partner**

## The task

Take one task you do **at least monthly** and design how AI will help with it:
a **tested prompt template** plus a filled-in
**[use-case canvas](use-case-canvas.md)** you could show a manager, a
co-founder, or yourself.

## The finish line

By 15:25 you have:

- [ ] A task mapped into steps: AI does / AI helps, I decide / me only
- [ ] A template with `[CAPITAL SLOTS]` and its header: **Use this for** /
      **Don't use this for** / **Check before using the output**
- [ ] A log of at least **one** *what I changed → what happened* round (two is
      better)
- [ ] The canvas: value, data and policy, risks and checks, pilot plan
- [ ] Your partner has run the template and answered *"Would I approve this
      pilot?"*
- [ ] Posted to the shared document

## Rules

1. **Criteria before output.** No changing them after you've seen the result.
2. **One change per round.** If you change two things, you won't know which
   one helped.
3. **Count the checking time.** Time saved means time saved *after* reviewing
   the output.
4. **Nothing confidential.** Use realistic but general examples.

## Pitch at 15:30 (60 seconds per pair)

Pitch one canvas as if to the person who'd approve it: the task, the hours saved
each month, the biggest risk and its check, and the pilot.
